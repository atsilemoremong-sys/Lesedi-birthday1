★ Entry #01 ★

Title: How it all started

Posted: Somewhere in the beginning

I'm gonna make this one a little longer than just a little summary memory card. For this one, I want you to feel the same way I've felt this entire friendship. Let's go into the first step of our friendship.



So, first day of school, I'm bored. I didn't wanna come to school today, I was just slouchy — and then I heard I had to come in at 8? Great, my day was ruined.



Time passes and I'm now on the third floor, waiting for someone to tell me who my class teacher would be.

No one I was comfortable with was there with me and I had to sit there, bored and annoyed.



Suddenly my name gets called and I had to sit in a random classroom, waiting to be assigned to a teacher.

By this point I didn't bother waiting for anyone, but I had this strange feeling that this year I was gonna find someone.

Not sure who, or how important they'd be to me.



A couple hours pass, I'm seated in a classroom full of people I don't know and a teacher I didn't recognise. Using my phone in boredom, when out of nowhere I felt the sudden urge to look up as someone entered the door. I didn't look up as everyone else did, but then you walked in.

For some reason I knew you were the one. I just felt it. We laid eyes on one another and I felt this hidden spark.

That day, we sat next to each other and in like 30 seconds MAX we became best friends.

Looking back now, I think this was one of the best days of my life.

★ End of Entry #01 ★

★ Entry #02 ★

Title: Our best week

Posted: Somewhere in Form 3

This story is going to be a much longer explanation as to what I had written on the summary card. This story is about what happened in Kasane, in Form 3.



I remember our excitement before we even went. weeks before, planning all the fun things we'd do, the memories we would create together. I also remmeber one day, I came home from school, telling my father about it and he told me he could pay for it, I was devasted but roughly a week before we left my father gave me the exciting news I would be able to go. I can remember how happy we were that we'd go together.



The day comes, at a crisp 6.00 am, we were at school, luggage in hand. Our parents wished us good luck and that early morning we took off for Kasane. A 13-hour trip that would mark one of the best days of my life.



On that bus, we laughed, never bored of our prescence together, we even made a few friends that we would never talk to again *cough cough* (Teto). Our cameras pointed at every little thing that we found to be interesting. Funny stores we shared with one another.



As much as I want to give credit to anyone out there, you were the only one I could focus on, I am dearly greatful I can call you my best friend, Lesedi Gemma Graham

★ End of Entry #02 ★
